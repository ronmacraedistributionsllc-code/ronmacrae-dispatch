## Full description (Play ≤4000 / Apple)
Ronmacrae Dispatch runs a delivery operation from one app.

**For dispatchers and business owners**
- Book deliveries and see every order's status in one place
- Assign a courier, or broadcast an order and let the first available courier accept it
- Live map of your couriers while they are on shift
- Cash-on-delivery reconciliation: what each courier collected, handed in and still owes
- Reports and CSV export, courier search, team and permissions

**For couriers**
- See only the deliveries assigned to you, with pickup and drop-off details
- Confirm delivery with the customer's PIN and photo proof
- Your cash summary for the day

**For merchants and fleet companies**
- Your own sign-in to track your orders or your fleet
- Share a tracking link with your customer — no app needed for them

**For customers**
- Follow a delivery from a link and rate the courier afterwards

Accounts can be deleted from inside the app (My account → Delete my account) or at the Delete account page. Past orders stay on record for the business, as the privacy policy explains.
